# SerpBear documentation

SerpBear tracks where your websites rank on Google. It checks keywords through a SERP data provider on a schedule, keeps the history, flags map pack appearances, and can email you digests. This is the v-serpbear fork of [upstream SerpBear](https://github.com/towfiqi/serpbear).

These pages live in the `docs/` folder of the [main repository](https://github.com/djav1985/v-serpbear) and are published to this wiki automatically. Edit them there, not here: the next sync overwrites changes made in the wiki.
